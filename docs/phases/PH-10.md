# PH-10 — Operating policies in the product
Type: PHASE CONTEXT
Status: ACTIVE
Objective / Feature IDs: OBJ-SUP-03, OBJ-SUP-04; FEAT-CHAT, FEAT-STAFF, FEAT-ACCESS, FEAT-CASE, FEAT-NOTIFY
Cycle: 4 (count in the `ROADMAP.md` ledger)

## Outcome and why now
The operating policies the Owner decided on 2026-09-14 (DEC-0039, `../runbooks/OPERATIONS.md`) are what the product does, not a table of pending items: the human team's 24/7 schedule is the configuration, every time a customer sees is in their own time zone, a formal complaint is a topic the customer can choose and a supervisor's duty with a deadline, a customer's support data can be exported by an administrator on request and the export is recorded, and an access-recovery request is forwarded to the Verification team. Two policies stay open by the Owner's choice (retention — legal advice; the e-mail provider) and are not part of this phase.
Why now: the Owner answered the policy questions and asked to continue («continua», 2026-09-14); cycle 4 is at 0/3.

## Scope, non-goals and dependencies
In scope: (1) schedule and time zone — the decided 24/7 default, "24:00" as a closing time, availability and the outside-hours notice carrying instants so each customer reads them in their own zone, the all-day switch in the settings form; (2) formal complaints — topic `formal_complaint` ("Reclamação formal"), routing to supervisors, a 5-business-day deadline visible in supervision, staff reclassification, the category events kept; (3) exports and recovery — an admin-only export of one customer's support data (cases, public messages, attachment metadata, events) recorded with who asked for it and why; recovery requests forwarded to the Verification team by name, in the staff page and the customer's receipt.
Non-goals: the AI assistant (PH-11, BL-031); retention and deletion (Owner, legal advice); a real e-mail provider (Owner); a real staff directory or Orbit's verification process (BL-001); response targets (none decided).
Dependencies: PH-9 (approved), DEC-0039.

## Product rules and acceptance scenarios
- RULE-SUP-08: the customer's availability copy states the decided service (24 hours) and, when a supervisor narrows it, today's window and the next opening in the customer's zone — never a promise of response time.
- §10.1 honesty: an outside-hours notice names the next opening in the customer's zone.
- §5.4 / RULE-SUP-02: a formal complaint reaches a supervisor and shows its deadline; an agent cannot quietly take it.
- RULE-SUP-01 / RULE-SUP-09: an export contains the customer's own data only, is possible for an administrator only, and is recorded attributably.
- §4.5: a recovery request says which team takes it over.
Scenarios are listed per subphase.
