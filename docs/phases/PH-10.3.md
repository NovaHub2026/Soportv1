# PH-10.3 — Exports on request and recovery to Verification; phase closure
Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL PLAN
Status: APPROVED
Parent: `PH-10.md`
Feature context: `../features/FEAT-ACCESS/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-STAFF/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-CASE/CONTEXT.md`

## Objective, prerequisites and scope
Make DEC-0039 (h) and (i) the product's behaviour and close PH-10: an administrator exports one customer's support data on the customer's request and the export is recorded; an access-recovery request is forwarded to the Verification team by name. Prerequisite: PH-10.2. Out of scope: a self-service download by the customer (the Owner chose "admin only, on request"), deletion or retention (pending legal advice), Orbit's real verification process (BL-001).

## Affected boundaries and implementation approach
- Shared: `dataExportRequestSchema { reason }`, `DataExportRecord`, `CustomerDataExport` (the record, the customer's preferences, each case as the customer sees it plus its attributable history); `RECOVERY_HANDLING_TEAM = "verification"`, `AccessRecoveryReceipt.handlingTeam`, `AccessRecoveryRequest.forwardedTo`; the simulated directory gains an administrator (`staff-dani`, "Dani Alves") because exports are theirs alone.
- API: `data_exports` table (migration `0022`); `DataExportService.exportCustomer` (admin only → 403 `admin_required`; a malformed customer id → 400) builds the export from `CasesService.exportCase` (the customer projection: public messages with attachment metadata, the events, the record — never internal notes or consultations) and `NotificationJob.preferences`, then records who asked, for whom, why and how many cases; `DataExportService.list` (admin) reads the record; `POST /api/staff/customers/:customerId/export`, `GET /api/staff/data-exports`. Recovery: receipts carry `handlingTeam`, forwarded requests carry `forwardedTo`.
- Web: `DataExportSection` in supervision for administrators (customer id + reason → the browser downloads the JSON; the recorded exports listed below); recovery copy names the Verification team on the customer's receipt, the staff page's intro, the forward button and its hint, and the "forwarded" status.

## Required behavior, failures and acceptance evidence
- A supervisor's or an agent's export → 403; an administrator's → 201 with the record (customer, admin, reason, case count) and the data; a short reason or a malformed id → 400.
- The export holds the customer's own cases with public messages and events, their preferences, and nothing of another customer, no internal note and no consultation; the customer projection's staff-only fields are absent; a customer without cases exports an empty, recorded set with default preferences.
- The record lists the export; supervisors cannot read the record.
- A receipt says `handlingTeam: "verification"`; a request is `forwardedTo: null` until forwarded, then `"verification"`.
- Web: a supervisor has no export section; an administrator exports with a reason (required), sees the recorded export and its list; the staff recovery page forwards "à equipe de Verificação".
Acceptance evidence: `../evidence/PH-10.3-verification.md`; phase: `../evidence/PH-10-phase-approval.md`.
