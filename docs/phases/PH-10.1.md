# PH-10.1 — Schedule and time zone
Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL PLAN
Status: APPROVED
Parent: `PH-10.md`
Feature context: `../features/FEAT-CHAT/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-STAFF/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-NOTIFY/CONTEXT.md`

## Objective, prerequisites and scope
Make DEC-0039 (a) the product's behaviour: the human team works 24/7, the schedule is kept in São Paulo time, and every customer reads availability and openings in their own time zone. Prerequisite: PH-9. Out of scope: staff timestamps (they stay in the browser's zone), the AI first line.

## Affected boundaries and implementation approach
- `packages/shared/src/settings.ts`: `DEFAULT_SUPPORT_SETTINGS` is the decided policy (every day 00:00–24:00, São Paulo); "24:00" is a valid closing time (never an opening one); `isAllDay`; `zonedInstant(day, "HH:MM", zone)` (offset search through `Intl`, two passes, daylight saving included); `Availability` gains `alwaysOpen`, `todayWindow { opensAt, closesAt }` and `nextOpeningAt` as ISO instants; `nextOpening` stays for older readers and is null when always open.
- API: the outside-hours notice stores `nextOpeningAt` in `systemData` next to the operation's weekday and time (no migration — `system_data` is JSON).
- Web: `localOpening(iso)` and `customerTimeZone()` in `apps/web/src/i18n/index.ts`; the home says "Atendimento 24 horas, todos os dias." when always open, otherwise today's window and the next opening formatted by the browser, always with "Horários no seu fuso (<zone>)"; the working-default note is gone (the defaults are the Owner's decision, not the Agent's invention); `system-messages.ts` words a notice from `nextOpeningAt` when present and from the stored weekday otherwise; the settings form has a "Dia inteiro" switch per day (time inputs cannot hold "24:00").
- Tests: the api unit and e2e setups keep the always-open schedule as 00:00–24:00 (the closed 23:59 minute of FND-0051 is gone).

## Required behavior, failures and acceptance evidence
- The default availability is open, always open, with no next opening; a narrowed Saturday makes the home show "aberto/fechado agora" with the window in the customer's zone.
- Instants: Wednesday 09:00–18:00 in São Paulo → 12:00–21:00 UTC; the next opening Thursday 09:00 → 12:00 UTC; Madrid 09:00 in September → 07:00 UTC; "24:00" São Paulo → 03:00 UTC next day; "24:00" refused as an opening time; "24:01" refused.
- A notice with `nextOpeningAt` is worded in the customer's zone; one without keeps the operation's weekday and time.
Acceptance evidence: `../evidence/PH-10.1-verification.md`.

## Work performed and important decisions
As planned. DEC-0040 records the representation (24:00 closing, instants in the contract, the browser as the customer's zone until a real session says otherwise).
