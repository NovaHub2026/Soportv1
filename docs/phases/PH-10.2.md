# PH-10.2 — Formal complaints
Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL PLAN
Status: APPROVED
Parent: `PH-10.md`
Feature context: `../features/FEAT-CASE/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-STAFF/CONTEXT.md`, `../features/FEAT-CHAT/CONTEXT.md`

## Objective, prerequisites and scope
Make DEC-0039 (g) the product's behaviour: a formal complaint is a topic the customer can choose, it is a supervisor's duty, it carries an internal deadline of five business days, and staff may reclassify a case in either direction. Prerequisite: PH-10.1. Out of scope: a customer-facing promise of the deadline (the Owner decided it is internal), holiday calendars, notifications about the deadline.

## Affected boundaries and implementation approach
- Shared: category `formal_complaint` ("Reclamação formal"); `COMPLAINT_DEADLINE_BUSINESS_DAYS = 5`; `businessDaysAfter(from, days, zone)` (Monday–Friday in the operation's zone, same wall-clock time, a weekend start counts from Monday); `CaseSummary.complaintDeadlineAt` (staff-only, stripped from customer surfaces); `SupervisionOverview.complaints { count, overdue, list }`; `staffMayWorkComplaint(role)` — the one rule the API and the web share: agents may not take, reply, note, change state, consult, edit or transfer a complaint; answering a consultation stays open to the specialist asked.
- API: `support_cases.complaint_deadline_at` and the enum value (migration `0021`); the deadline is set at creation for a customer-chosen complaint, inherited by a follow-up, set when staff reclassify into the category and cleared when they reclassify out (the `category_changed` event is kept); `assertMayWorkComplaint` in every staff action (403 `supervisor_required`); a transfer target must be a supervisor or admin (`StaffDirectory.roleOf`, 400 `supervisor_required`); the operation's time zone is read before a transaction opens (a query inside one deadlocks PGlite's single connection — found by the unit tests); supervision lists open complaints by deadline and counts the ones past it.
- Web: the topic chip with a note under it (supervisor, five business days); the queue tag and deadline (or "Prazo vencido há …"); the case view shows the tag and deadline, and for an agent hides "Assumir caso", the actions and the composer with the supervisor-only note; supervision gets a "Reclamações formais" section with the summary and the list; `isPast` in `i18n` keeps the clock out of components (React purity lint).

## Required behavior, failures and acceptance evidence
- A customer-chosen complaint has a deadline 5–9 calendar days after creation (five business days), invisible to the customer; its follow-up is a complaint with its own deadline.
- An agent's take, reply, note, status change, attribute edit and consultation on a complaint → 403 `supervisor_required` and the case stays unassigned; a supervisor takes it, replies and consults a team, and the specialist (an agent) answers; handing it to an agent → 400.
- Reclassifying out clears the deadline and lets agents work the case; reclassifying in sets a fresh deadline.
- Supervision: complaints listed by deadline, the ones past it counted; a resolved one leaves the list.
- HTTP: the customer's response has no `complaintDeadlineAt`; the strict contracts still hold; the overview carries `complaints`.
- Web: six topic chips, the complaint note on selection; an agent sees the complaint locked; a supervisor sees the section and can open a complaint from it.
