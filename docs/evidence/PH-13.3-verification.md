## Limitations
- The broker is simulated: it answers only the endpoints our adapters call, with the frontend's view of the shapes and fictional data; this proves the mechanics of the integration, not a connection (DEC-0047, §14 item 10). The real run is PH-13.4.
- The iframe hand-off was exercised by the checks at the HTTP level (host page served, `/embed` rendered, bearer flow) and by reading the host page's script; no Playwright run drove the two windows.
- Staff are simulated (`x-simulated-staff-id`); the staff directory is the simulated list, so the simulated broker's team members are not what transfers are validated against in this demo.
